Five ways to sink a plane

Five directions for the app icon and wordmark. Each one is shown as the iOS icon, at the sizes iOS really draws it, through Android's circle mask, as an Android 13+ themed icon, and as a wordmark on light and dark backgrounds. Nosedive (concept 1) was picked; make-icons.py builds the real icon set from it.

After you pick one

  1. Refine the chosen mark: line weights, optical centring, and a check at 29pt on a real device.
  2. Export the full set: icon.png (1024), Android adaptive foreground, background and monochrome layers, the splash icon, and Play's 512 × 512 icon.
  3. Replace Expo's template icon in app.json, and update the store feature graphic and caption frames to match.

Wordmark faces are all from Google Fonts (SIL Open Font License), so they're free to use in a logo.
