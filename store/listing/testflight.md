# TestFlight: "What to Test"

Paste into App Store Connect › TestFlight › the build › Test Details. Update per build.

## First build

```
Thanks for testing Sinkplane! You'll need a Floatplane account with an active subscription.

Please try:
1. Sign in with your Floatplane username and password (and your 2FA code if you use one). The "verify you are human" check usually passes on its own.
2. Videos: scroll the feed, switch channels, change the sort order, pull to refresh.
3. Playing a video: double-tap left/right to skip 15s, press and hold for 2× speed, try the speed menu, rotate the phone for fullscreen, seek with the bar.
4. Leave a video halfway and reopen it: it should resume where you stopped (also from the History tab).
5. Tap the down arrow in the player: the video keeps playing in the mini player. Drag it to another corner, tap it to go back.
6. Lock the phone or go to the Home Screen while a video plays: it should keep playing (picture-in-picture or audio).
7. Live tab: open a creator who is live.
8. Settings: switch the theme, turn on notifications, download a video, then turn on Airplane Mode and play it from the Videos tab.
9. On iPad: rotate it, and check the grid layouts.

Known gaps:
- New-post notifications arrive while the app is open, or from a background check about every 15 minutes (iOS decides when). They're not instant push notifications.

Found a bug? Take a screenshot in the app and TestFlight will offer to send it with a comment, or file it at https://github.com/Sinkplane/sinkplane-mobile/issues (please leave out your password and session token).
```
