# Sinkplane privacy policy

_Last updated: November 18, 2026_

Sinkplane is an independent, open-source app for watching your Floatplane subscriptions. It's made by
Kingdomworks LLC ("we"). It isn't affiliated with Floatplane Media Inc.

## What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers for the app, and Sinkplane contains no analytics, advertising,
tracking or crash-reporting code. We never see your account details, what you watch, or anything else
you do in the app.

## What the app sends to Floatplane

Sinkplane talks directly to Floatplane (floatplane.com), the same way Floatplane's own apps and
website do:

- Your username or email, password and two-factor code when you sign in, plus Cloudflare's
  "verify you are human" check that Floatplane requires.
- Requests for your subscriptions, videos, comments, live streams and watch history.
- Your watch progress, likes and dislikes, and notification settings.

Floatplane handles this data under its own privacy policy: https://www.floatplane.com/legal/privacy

## What stays on your device

- Your Floatplane session token, kept in the system's secure storage (iOS Keychain or Android
  Keystore).
- Videos you download, and copies of the video pages you've opened so they work offline.
- Your settings (theme, playback speed, notification preferences).

Signing out deletes the session token and the saved video pages. Deleting the app deletes everything.
Downloaded videos you chose to save to your photo library stay there until you delete them.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**: to tell you when a creator you follow posts something new. Optional.
- **Photo library (add only)**: to save a downloaded video to your library, only when you ask.

## Children

Sinkplane isn't directed at children under 13, and a Floatplane account is required to use it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
